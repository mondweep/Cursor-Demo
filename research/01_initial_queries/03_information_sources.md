# Information Sources for Marine Plastic Pollution Education App Research

## Scientific and Research Organizations

1. **United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP)**
   - Provides authoritative data on global plastic pollution
   - Publishes regular reports on the state of plastic pollution
   - Website: [unep.org](https://www.unep.org)

2. **National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA)**
   - Research on marine debris and plastic pollution impacts
   - Educational resources for teaching about marine environments
   - Website: [marinedebris.noaa.gov](https://marinedebris.noaa.gov)

3. **The Ocean Cleanup**
   - Research on plastic pollution in oceans and rivers
   - Innovative approaches to cleanup and prevention
   - Website: [theoceancleanup.com](https://theoceancleanup.com)

4. **Plastic Soup Foundation**
   - Focus on plastic pollution education and prevention
   - Developers of "My Little Plastic Footprint" app
   - Website: [plasticsoupfoundation.org](https://www.plasticsoupfoundation.org)

5. **Ocean Conservancy**
   - Annual International Coastal Cleanup data
   - Research on plastic pollution impacts on marine life
   - Website: [oceanconservancy.org](https://oceanconservancy.org)

## Academic Journals and Publications

2. **Science of the Total Environment**

   - Access: Academic databases or journal website

# Key Research Questions: Marine Plastic Pollution Education App

## Understanding the Problem Space

1. What are the most current and alarming statistics regarding plastic pollution in marine environments?
2. Which marine species are most affected by plastic pollution, and what are the mechanisms of harm?
3. What are the primary sources of plastic pollution entering marine ecosystems?
4. What are the most common misconceptions about plastic pollution that need to be addressed?
5. How does plastic pollution variably impact different marine ecosystems (coral reefs, deep sea, coastal areas)?

## Educational Approaches

1. What teaching methodologies are most effective for environmental education with children and young adults?
2. How can complex environmental issues be simplified without losing scientific accuracy?
3. What visual approaches work best for representing environmental data to non-specialists?
4. How can educational content balance the severity of the problem while avoiding eco-anxiety?
5. What narrative techniques can make environmental education more engaging and memorable?

## Mobile Application Design

1. What gamification elements have proven most effective in environmental education apps?
2. What features drive the highest user engagement and retention in educational applications?
3. How can progress tracking be implemented to maintain user motivation?
4. What interactive elements best facilitate learning about environmental issues?
5. How can user-generated content be incorporated to enhance engagement?
6. What are the most successful examples of environmental education apps, and what makes them effective?

## Behavior Change

1. How can digital applications effectively translate awareness into real-world action?
2. What incentive structures most effectively drive sustainable behavior change?
3. How can social features amplify individual environmental actions?
4. What metrics can accurately measure the impact of educational interventions on behavior?
5. How can challenges and achievements be structured to promote ongoing environmental stewardship?

## Technical Implementation

1. What are the essential features that can be realistically implemented in a 2-hour hackathon?
2. Which technical frameworks or libraries are best suited for rapid development of an educational app?
3. How can environmental data be efficiently visualized with limited development time?
4. What free or open-source resources are available for environmental education content?
5. How can the app be designed to be easily expandable after the initial hackathon implementation?

## Target Audience Analysis

1. What age groups are most receptive to educational interventions about marine pollution?
2. How do motivations for environmental action differ across demographics?
3. What interface design considerations are important for different age groups?
4. How can the app appeal to both educational and entertainment interests?
5. What barriers might prevent engagement with an environmental education app?

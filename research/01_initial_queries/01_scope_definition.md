# Scope Definition: Marine Plastic Pollution Education App

## Project Overview
This research aims to guide the development of an educational mobile application focused on raising awareness about plastic pollution in marine environments. The application will target children and young people, making complex environmental issues accessible through gamification and interactive features.

## Core Research Areas

1. **Current State of Plastic Pollution**
   - Global statistics and trends in plastic waste generation and disposal
   - Impact on marine ecosystems and wildlife
   - Regional variations in plastic pollution severity

2. **Educational Approaches**
   - Effective methods for environmental education in digital formats
   - Age-appropriate content development for environmental topics
   - Engagement strategies for sustaining interest in environmental issues

3. **Mobile Application Features**
   - Gamification elements for environmental education
   - Interactive visualization of environmental data
   - User engagement and retention mechanisms
   - Similar applications in the market and their strengths/weaknesses

4. **Behavior Change Mechanisms**
   - Strategies to transform awareness into action
   - Measuring and incentivizing positive environmental behaviors
   - Community-building approaches for collective environmental action

5. **Technical Implementation Considerations**
   - Platform requirements and constraints for a 2-hour hackathon
   - Rapid prototyping techniques for educational applications
   - Essential vs. nice-to-have features

## Research Boundaries

This research will focus specifically on plastic pollution in marine environments rather than all forms of pollution or environmental issues. While global in perspective, particular attention will be given to solutions that can be implemented within a short development timeframe suitable for a hackathon environment.

The research will not include:
- Detailed technical specifications for implementation
- Complete content creation for the application
- Business model or monetization strategies
- Marketing plan for the application

## Expected Outcomes

This research aims to provide:
- Evidence-based insights to inform the application design
- Prioritized feature recommendations for maximum educational impact
- Guidance on effective engagement strategies for the target audience
- Reference examples of successful approaches in similar applications
